Your Mental Performance Profile

This is the Test of Performance Strategies (TOPS‑2), a published, validated sport-psychology questionnaire. It measures how often you already use skills such as goal-setting, self-talk, imagery, and relaxation, in training and in competition, giving us a specific baseline to build the rest of the work from.

Length64 statements, ~12–15 min
FormatTwo parts: Training, then Competition
Scale1 Never → 5 Always
Before you start

There are no right or wrong answers here, only honest ones. Answer based on how you actually train and compete right now, not how you wish you did. Go with your first instinct rather than overthinking any one item.

What happens to your answers

Your report is generated right here in your browser once you finish, and a copy is sent to your coach automatically. You'll also get your own copy on screen, with the option to download it as a PDF.

Add your name above first, so your coach knows whose report this is.

Adapted from the Test of Performance Strategies (Thomas, Murphy & Hardy, 1999) and its 2010 factor-refined version, TOPS‑2 (Hardy, Roberts, Thomas & Murphy, Psychology of Sport and Exercise).
